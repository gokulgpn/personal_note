---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Python package for delineating field boundaries. Automates agricultural field boundary delineation from satellite imagery using a unified framework combining seven approaches (object detection, semantic segmentation, vision transformers, foundation models, embedding-based clustering, fine-tuning, and multi-engine ensembling). Pulls composites from Google Earth Engine or local GeoTIFFs and automatically removes non-agricultural polygons using land cover datasets (NLCD, Dynamic World, C3S) so results represent actual crop fields rather than roads or buildings. Keywords: field boundaries, satellite imagery, Google Earth Engine, segmentation, foundation models, precision agriculture. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Python]]
