# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite imagery, earth observation products, and geospatial analysis techniques.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

---

- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Article comparing different earth embeddings. Examines how geospatial foundation model teams excel at the hard problem of training models on petabytes of satellite imagery, but consistently fail at the easier problem of standardizing how the resulting embeddings are distributed and accessed — each product uses different file formats, coordinate systems, and spatial resolutions, forcing downstream users to write custom integration code. Keywords: earth embeddings, foundation models, geospatial, interoperability, satellite imagery, technical debt. Related: [[Embeddings]], [[Machine_Learning]]
- See [[Urban_Planning]] for a note critiquing the use of satellite land surface temperature (LST) as a proxy for the heat people actually feel.
