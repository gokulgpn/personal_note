- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): An open global dataset of 2.6 million historical flood events derived from news articles, spanning more than 150 countries. Published February 2026 by a team of 15 researchers (including Mayo, Zlydenko, Kratzert, and others) and released under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Keywords: flood events, dataset, disaster data, news mining, hydrology, open data. Related: [[Data]]
